Meet Awuraama Dadson

Author of The Master Manifester · Digital Product Enterpreneur · Software & Chemical Engineer · Manifestation & Personal Development Content Creator

I’m Awuraama Dadson, an author, creator, and lifelong student of growth, mindset, and intentional living. I’m fascinated by the journey of becoming—the process of transforming who we are, challenging the beliefs that limit us, and building a life that feels aligned with who we truly desire to be. My work sits at the intersection of personal development, faith, self-concept, creativity, and the pursuit of a meaningful life. I love exploring ideas around mindset, manifestation, abundance, and the power of belief, while also sharing the lessons I’ve learned along my own journey of growth. Beyond writing, I’m passionate about creating things that inspire transformation—from digital products and businesses to content that encourages people to think differently about their potential. I’m also constantly learning, exploring technology, and expanding my understanding of how creativity and innovation can be used to build a better future. My first book, *The Master Manifester*, is a reflection of my six-year exploration into manifestation, belief, faith, and the deeper principles behind creating change from within. Through my writing and creative work, my hope is to encourage others to embrace their own becoming journey; to grow, believe bigger, and intentionally create a life they are proud of.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is The Master Manifester about?

The Master Manifester: The Mastery Blueprint for Manifesting Anything Like a Pro is a guide to understanding and applying the principles of manifestation in your everyday life.

Drawing on the Law of Assumption, self-concept, faith in God, and practical exercises, this book explores how your assumptions about yourself and your life influence the way you think, feel, and act. It is designed to help you become more intentional about your inner world and the life you're creating.

Whether you're new to manifestation or have been practising for years, this book offers a framework to deepen your understanding and put what you learn into practice.

Is the book fiction or nonfiction?

The Master Manifester is a nonfiction personal development book. It combines explanations, personal insights, biblical perspectives, and practical exercises to help you explore manifestation and apply its principles to your life. The book also begins with my personal story and journey, offering a glimpse into the experiences that shaped my understanding of manifestation.

Who is this book for?

This book is for anyone interested in personal growth, manifestation, self-concept, and creating a more intentional life.

It's especially suited to readers who want to explore the Law of Assumption, understand their beliefs about themselves more deeply, and learn about manifestation from a perspective that also makes room for faith in God.

You don't need to be an expert in manifestation to get started. All you need is an open mind and a willingness to explore the ideas presented in the book.

Do I need prior knowledge of manifestation to read it?

Not at all! The book is suitable for beginners as well as those who already have experience with manifestation.

Whether you're just discovering concepts such as the Law of Assumption and self-concept or looking to deepen your existing understanding, you can work through the ideas at your own pace and revisit the exercises whenever you need to.

Does the book incorporate the Bible?

Yes! Faith in God and biblical insights are an important part of the book's approach.

The Master Manifester explores manifestation alongside spiritual reflection and biblical perspectives, discussing concepts such as self-concept, assumptions, and abundance. It's written for readers who want to explore these ideas while making room for their relationship with God.

Is manifestation about pretending that problems don't exist?

Not at all. Manifestation doesn't have to mean denying your current circumstances or pretending that difficult things never happen.

The book encourages you to examine the assumptions and beliefs you hold about yourself and your possibilities, while becoming more intentional about your mindset and responses. You can acknowledge where you are right now while remaining open to the possibility of a different future.

Is this book suitable for beginners?

Absolutely! If you've come across terms such as Law of Assumption, self-concept, or living in the end and want to understand them better, this book is a great place to begin.

You can work through the concepts at your own pace and return to the practical exercises as you continue learning and developing your understanding.

Will reading this book guarantee that I manifest everything I want?

While the book provides insights, perspectives, and practical exercises to support your personal development and manifestation practice, individual experiences and outcomes will naturally vary.

The intention is to help you deepen your understanding of manifestation, become more conscious of your assumptions, and approach your life with greater intentionality. The book is a resource for learning and personal growth rather than a guarantee of specific outcomes.
